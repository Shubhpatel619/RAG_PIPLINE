# Billing FAQ

**What plans does Aperture offer?**
Free (1 workspace, 30-day snapshot retention, no historical backfill), Team ($15/seat/month, 400-day retention, 90-day backfill), and Enterprise (custom pricing, indefinite retention, SSO, and audit logs).

**How is a "seat" counted?**
A seat is any workspace member who can log in and view a dashboard, regardless of whether they use it that month. Deactivated members do not count toward the seat total starting the billing cycle after deactivation.

**Can I switch plans mid-cycle?**
Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated for the remainder of the billing cycle. Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing cycle; you keep your current plan's features until then.

**What happens to my data if I downgrade from Team to Free?**
Snapshots older than 30 days are deleted 7 days after the downgrade takes effect, not immediately. You'll see a warning banner in the dashboard during that 7-day window.

**Do you offer a nonprofit or education discount?**
Yes, 30% off Team plan pricing for verified nonprofits and educational institutions. This is arranged manually with support and is not available for Enterprise.

**Is there a free trial for Team or Enterprise?**
Team includes a 14-day trial with no card required. Enterprise trials are arranged individually through sales.
